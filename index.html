Weight Thing

A simple, private app for tracking your weight and what you eat.

What it does

  • Record your weight and see it on a graph over time.
  • Log the foods you eat by scanning a barcode or entering them manually.
  • Keeps your records on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
