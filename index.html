Weight Thing

A private app for tracking your weight and what you eat.

In short: Weight Thing keeps your weight and food records on your own device. I don't collect them, there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only thing that leaves your device is the barcode number of a food when you ask the app to look it up.

What it does

  • Records your weight and lets you see it on a graph over time.
  • Log the foods you eat by scanning a barcode or entering them manually.
  • Shows nutritional information for the foods you log.
  • Shows nutritional information per serving, per meal and per day.
  • Keeps your records private on your own device.

What it doesn't do

  • No accounts.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics.
  • No cookies.
  • No subscription.
