Weight Thing – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short: Weight Thing keeps your weight and food records on your own device. I don't collect them, there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only thing that leaves your device is the barcode number of a food when you ask the app to look it up.

This policy explains what information the Weight Thing app (“the app”) handles and how. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer (“I”, “me”) based in Scotland, UK.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in a private database on your device, so that it can show your graph and food log:

  • Your weight entries (date and weight).
  • Your food log (date, time, food name, amount, calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, salt and meal).
  • Foods you choose to remember, including barcode numbers, names, brands and nutrition values.
  • Your settings, such as your preferred weight unit.

This information stays on your device. I do not have access to it, and it is not sent to me or to any server I run. I do not run any servers for this app.

Because the data is only on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage will permanently delete it. I cannot recover it for you.

2. Information I collect

None. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, location or contact details. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it does not use cookies.

3. Camera and photos

  • Camera: the app asks for camera permission only so you can scan food barcodes. Images are processed on your device to read the barcode number and are not saved or sent anywhere. You can use the app without granting this permission by typing the barcode or entering the food manually.
  • Photos: if you choose “from a photo”, the app reads only the photo you pick, on your device, to find a barcode. The photo is not stored or uploaded.

4. Internet access and Open Food Facts

When you scan or enter a barcode and ask the app to look it up, the app sends that barcode number over the internet to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to fetch the product’s name, brand and nutrition information. The request contains the barcode number and the app’s name and version (as a standard “user agent”). As with any internet request, Open Food Facts will necessarily receive your device’s IP address. No weight data, food log entries or personal details are sent.

Open Food Facts is a separate service with its own privacy policy, available on their website. If you don’t want to use the lookup, you can enter foods manually and the app works offline.

5. Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, share or transfer your information to anyone, because I do not have it. The only third-party contact is the barcode lookup described above.

6. Your control over your data

All of your data is in your hands. You can edit or delete any weight or food entry inside the app, and you can delete everything at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. You can revoke the camera permission in your device’s settings at any time.

7. Security

Your data is held in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot normally access. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and the app does not add its own encryption, so I recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.

8. Health information

Weight and food records can be considered health-related. Weight Thing is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it does not provide medical advice. Please consult a doctor or other qualified health professional before making changes to your diet, exercise or weight. Because this information never leaves your device (apart from the barcode lookup), I do not process it.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the project’s GitHub page: github.com/TheBaldyMan/weight-thing/issues. You will need a free GitHub account to do so.
